# 👋 Hi, I'm Viraj Punjani

Welcome to my GitHub space!

I’m a cybersecurity graduate and practitioner who’s passionate about protecting people and organizations by building secure systems. My journey started with a Diploma in Computer Science Engineering, where I became deeply curious about ethical hacking and how cyber threats operate in the real world.

That curiosity evolved into experience during my internship and training at Krydence, where I explored mobile/wireless security, bug hunting, cybercrime investigation, and web application testing. During this time, I earned a spot on the Bug Bounty Hall of Fame from bug-crowd platform for responsibly disclosing web application vulnerabilities in Blend and Mailgun—rooted in OWASP Top 10 flaws. This solidified my drive to pursue cybersecurity professionally.

At La Trobe University, I completed a Bachelor of Cybersecurity, where I applied my learning through real-world penetration tests, CTF challenges, and vulnerability analysis on live systems—including testing my university's website and subdomains. I gained experience with tools like Wireshark, Metasploit, Nessus, Kali Linux & other distributions, Jira, and Confluence.

Through my WBL and capstone units, I led two industry projects:
- A secure server migration project for Captivate Connect, focusing on infrastructure upgrades and cloud-based risk mitigation  
- A full risk assessment and policy alignment report for Arrow Leadership, delivering awareness training to non-technical staff

I was also selected as the first intern in La Trobe’s internal SOC team, where I worked for six months with Microsoft Defender 365, Sentinel, and ServiceNow. I created detection runbooks for phishing, BEC, malware, and login anomalies, and escalated critical incidents to analysts. I have achieved 30% reduction in false positive alerts through historical log analysis & fine-tuned detection rules using Kusto Query Language. I also supported compliance efforts by aligning daily operations with the university’s security governance framework.

Currently, I’m building deeper technical fluency through certifications like the Google Cybersecurity Certificate, Microsoft SC-200, Azure Fundamentals, and BTL1, while continuously working on detection engineering, cloud defense, and GRC fundamentals.

Thanks for stopping by—scroll below to check out some of my project work, hands-on labs, and tools I’ve worked with.
